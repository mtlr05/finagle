# Example Pump Co. (XMPL): A Steady Compounder at 9x EBITDA

*A fictional article for the finagle agent example. The company, its figures, and its management are invented.*

Published 2024-08-05

Example Pump Co. trades at $42 per share as of August 1, 2024, with 50 million shares outstanding. All figures are in US dollars.

## Fiscal 2023

For fiscal 2023, the year ended December 31, 2023, revenue was $1,200 million and adjusted EBITDA was $240 million. Adjusted EBITDA adds back $12 million of stock-based compensation. Capital expenditure was $36 million, depreciation and amortization was $40 million, cash taxes were $38 million, and interest expense was $22 million. At year end the balance sheet carried $400 million of debt and $90 million of cash.

The first half of 2024 was solid too: revenue for the twelve months ended June 30, 2024 reached $1,250 million.

## Outlook

Management guided to EBITDA growth of 8% in 2024 and 6% in 2025. Capex should run about $40 million in each of 2024 and 2025 as the new foundry comes online.

## Capital allocation

The board authorized buybacks of $100 million a year for 2024 through 2026. The dividend is $0.40 per share a year. Management targets leverage of 1.5x EBITDA starting in 2025.

The company agreed to buy a small valve maker for $90 million, about 9 times EBITDA, closing in 2024 and paid for with cash.

## Valuation

I discount equity cash flows at 9%. My price target is $55.
